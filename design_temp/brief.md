# Design Brief: RdmWebTech Premium Homepage

## Objective
Create a high-converting, premium homepage for "RdmWebTech", a modern web design agency. The goal is to project authority, innovation, and technical excellence to small business owners and startups.

## Aesthetic Direction: "Neon Noir / Cyber-Premium"
- **Mood**: Sleek, futuristic, professional, and high-tech.
- **Visuals**: Dark mode base with vibrant neon accents (Cyan/Purple). Glassmorphic cards with subtle backdrops. Clean, spacious layouts with high-contrast typography.
- **Animations**: Smooth scroll-triggered reveals, hovering card lifts, and subtle glowing pulse effects on CTAs.

## Content Structure
1. **Nav**: Sticky glassmorphic navbar with Logo (RdmWebTech) and navigation links.
2. **Hero**: 
   - Headline: "Transform Your Business with High-Performance Digital Experiences."
   - Subheadline: "We build premium responsive websites, ecommerce stores, and landing pages that turn visitors into loyal customers."
   - CTA: "Start Your Project" (Gradient Button), "View Work" (Outline Button).
3. **Problem/Solution**: "The Digital Gap" - Contrast between slow, dated websites and RdmWebTech's optimized solutions.
4. **Services Grid**: 
   - Responsive Business Sites
   - Ecommerce Engines
   - Lead Gen Landing Pages
   - Portfolio Showcases
5. **Why Choose Us**: Performance, SEO-Ready, Mobile-First, 24/7 Support.
6. **Portfolio**: A showcase of 3-4 stunning web projects in modern mockups.
7. **Testimonials**: Glass cards from satisfied business owners.
8. **Pricing**: 
   - Starter ($499)
   - Professional ($1499)
   - Enterprise ($2999+)
9. **FAQ**: Accordions for common questions (Timeline, SEO, Hosting).
10. **Final CTA**: "Ready to Launch Your Next Project?"
11. **Footer**: Contact details (8179479516, Ramana9000r@gmail.com) and social links.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Lexend (Bold/ExtraBold) for a modern, geometric look.
- **Body**: Inter (Regular/Medium) for maximum readability.

## Color Palette
- **Base**: #0B0E14 (Deep Charcoal)
- **Primary Accent**: #00B7FF (Electric Cyan)
- **Secondary Accent**: #7000FF (Neon Purple)
- **Text**: #F8FAFC (Slate White)
- **Glass**: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) with backdrop-filter: blur(12px).

## Technical Requirements
- Use **Tailwind CSS** for styling.
- Use **Framer Motion** style CSS animations (or raw CSS keyframes).
- Mobile-responsive (Grid/Flexbox).
- Include Lucide-style icons.

## Output Path
- `.\index.html` (Update the existing one)

## Image Needs
- Abstract 3D "Digital Grid" hero background.
- 4x Modern service icons (glass style).
- 3x Website mockups for portfolio.
- 3x Professional user avatars.
